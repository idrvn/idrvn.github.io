Note 01

IDRVN carries over fifty years of applied arts and heritage design practice in India, including work for major hospitality and cultural institutions, into a studio shaped by a decade of architectural planning and delivery in Germany. One generation built the vocabulary. The next is translating it for buildings, brands, and archives that don't exist yet.

The studio's research method comes from Vernacular Branding, a study of South Asia's visual identity landscape, extended here into spatial design, brand strategy, and digital practice.
